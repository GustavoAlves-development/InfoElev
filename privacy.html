Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect the information you share with us.

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Infoelev Digital Business LLC ("Infoelev," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or purchase our digital products, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide directly: your name, email address, and billing details when you make a purchase, and any information you submit through our contact form.
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected through standard web technologies when you browse our site.
  • Payment information: processed directly by our third-party payment processor. Infoelev does not store your full card number.
  • Member account information: when you buy a product we create a member account with your email address and a password, which we store only in hashed (one-way encrypted) form. We also keep your purchase history, access status, and login activity (date of last login and number of logins) to deliver the products and provide support.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process your purchase and deliver the digital product you bought;
  • To respond to questions and support requests submitted through our contact form;
  • To send transactional messages related to your purchase (such as receipts or delivery links);
  • To improve our website, products, and overall customer experience;
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations.

We do not use your information for automated profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Cookies

When you log in to your member account, we set a strictly necessary session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days; it is removed when you log out. Your progress inside the member apps is saved in your own browser's local storage.

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some site functionality.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the business, such as payment processors, email delivery services, and hosting providers, under obligations to protect your data;
  • Authorities, when required to comply with a legal obligation or valid legal process.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including to satisfy legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted (SSL/TLS) connections for data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us through our Contact page.

9. Children's Privacy

Our products and website are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International Visitors

Infoelev Digital Business LLC is based in the United States. If you access our website from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those of your home country.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please reach out via our Contact page. You can also email us at support@infoelev.com.
