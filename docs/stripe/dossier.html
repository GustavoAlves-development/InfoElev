04Repeat purchases and memberships

Side Hustle Sprints are designed to be bought more than once

A sprint is a fixed period — 7, 30, or 90 days — with one goal and a daily check-in in the member app. Each purchase adds one sprint credit to the customer's account; the credit is used when the customer starts the sprint. When a sprint ends, customers who want to keep going buy another one. Several sprint purchases from the same customer are therefore normal, expected activity.

  • Credits are counted on our server from the paid Stripe purchases; a sprint cannot be started without one.
  • A refunded sprint that has not been started is removed from the account automatically.
  • Sprints are not included in the Library or memberships, so customers only pay for the sprints they actually run.

Monthly memberships

Infoelev Tools ($9/month) and Infoelev All Access ($19/month) renew monthly until canceled. This is stated on the pricing cards, in our Terms, and on the Stripe Checkout page. There are no free trials and no automatic upgrades.

05Purchase and delivery flow

  1. Checkout. "Buy" or "Subscribe" opens Stripe Checkout for that exact product, created by our server. The checkout states that access is emailed right after payment and that the purchase is subject to our Terms and Refund Policy; memberships also state that they renew monthly until canceled.
  2. Confirmation page. After payment, the customer lands on a page that verifies the payment with Stripe and explains the next steps.
  3. Access email. A signed Stripe webhook creates the member account and emails the login, a generated password, and a link to the refund policy. Returning customers receive a "product added to your account" email instead.
  4. Member area. The customer logs in, sees their products, sprints, and purchase history, and can change or reset their password.
What the customer reads at checkout: "Instant digital access: your login is emailed right after payment. By purchasing you agree to our Terms of Use and Refund Policy at infoelev.com." Memberships add: "Renews monthly until you cancel."

06Member area and access control

Access is checked on our server before any product page or course content is served. Customers only see what they bought; Infoelev Tools members get the tools but not the course chapters. Refunds and membership cancellations remove the corresponding access automatically.

07Internal admin panel

Our admin panel lists every member account with what it can access and how that access was granted (Stripe purchase, or manual, such as the review account created for your team), each purchase's status (active, refunded, canceled), the account creation date, last login, and number of logins. It is restricted to Infoelev administrators.

Note on the screenshots. Because we have not processed customer payments yet, the member-area and admin screenshots show internal test accounts (example.com addresses), labeled as test accounts in the panel. They show exactly the screens customers and our team use.

09Notes for the reviewer

  • Educational content only. Every sales page, our Terms, and the checkout state that the products are educational, are not financial, tax, investment, or legal advice, and guarantee no income or financial outcome. No income screenshots or earnings claims are used.
  • Extra-income topics. The courses teach how to evaluate and start legitimate side work — freelancing, online gigs, reselling used items, and creating digital products — using the customer's own skills and time. None of them is a business opportunity, MLM, resale-rights, or "done-for-you" income scheme.
  • Repeat sprint purchases. Multiple purchases of Side Hustle Sprints by the same customer are the intended use (see section 04).
  • Memberships. Monthly pricing and "cancel anytime" are shown on the pricing cards and at checkout; renewal terms are in our Terms of Use.
  • No high-risk features. No free trials that convert silently, no pre-orders, no physical shipping, no third-party or white-label products.
  • Related business. The same management operates ElevPay LLC, a separate company selling personal-finance education with its own website, customers, bank account, and Stripe account.
